Membership registry

This is where Cosmic Explorer members sign up, keep their details current, and get access to the mailing lists, the document control center (DCC) and the collaboration's GitHub teams. You log in with your home institution's credentials, or with an ORCID iD if your institution is not federated.

Before you apply

You will be asked to log in first. Choose your home institution on the identity-provider page; if it is not listed, choose the ORCID option and sign in with your ORCID iD. Use your institutional e-mail address in the form, since that is where approval notices and mailing-list traffic go.

About Cosmic Explorer

Cosmic Explorer is a proposed next-generation gravitational-wave observatory in the United States. See cosmicexplorer.org for the science, the project and how to get involved.

Operated by the Cosmic Explorer Consortium. Membership data is managed with COmanage Registry.
